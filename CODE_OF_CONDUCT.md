# Code of conduct

cityImage follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions; harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Gabriele Filomena, at
gabriele.filomena@liverpool.ac.uk. Reports are handled confidentially, and the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant apply.
